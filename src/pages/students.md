It's easy to get involved with our lab. We are always looking for curious, motivated and enthusiastic students. If you'd like to join, here is how it works.

## Look through our research projects and topic areas

The research projects and topic areas give you a better idea of the work we do and what you would be doing if you joined. You don't need to already have the skills a project requires, and you don't need to know every topic.

* [Research projects](/research/)
* [List of topics](https://wiki.scel-hawaii.org/doku.php?id=about:topics)
* [Open positions](/open-positions/)

## Contact us

Once you've found a couple of positions that interest you, email [the current lab manager](/contact/) and include:

* Your name
* Your class standing
* Major and track, if you know
* Positions you are interested in
* Why you would like to join our lab

## Meet with us

* We will respond within a week and set up a time to meet. If you don't hear back within a couple of days, please email again, because it might be a busy time.
* The meeting is to get to know you and your interests, and to learn about your experience with EE-related projects and classes.
* Please bring:
  * A resume that includes your project experience and classes you have taken
  * Any questions you have for us
